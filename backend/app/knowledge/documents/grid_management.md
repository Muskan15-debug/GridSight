# Renewable Grid Management

Electricity systems must maintain a balance between generation and demand.

When renewable generation is lower than demand, additional energy may need
to come from energy storage or the electrical grid.

When renewable generation exceeds demand, surplus energy may be stored,
exported, or curtailed depending on available infrastructure and operating
conditions.

Forecasting renewable generation in advance allows operators to prepare for
expected energy deficits and surpluses.

A generation deficit combined with insufficient storage can increase expected
grid dependency.

A generation surplus combined with insufficient storage capacity can increase
the possibility of renewable energy being exported or curtailed.

Operational recommendations should therefore consider generation, demand,
storage availability, and the expected duration of the condition.
