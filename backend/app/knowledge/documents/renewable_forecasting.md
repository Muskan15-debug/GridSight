# Renewable Energy Forecasting

Renewable generation forecasts estimate future electricity production using
environmental and system variables.

Important variables for solar generation include:

- Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI)
- Direct Normal Irradiance (DNI)
- Diffuse Horizontal Irradiance (DHI)
- Ambient temperature
- Relative humidity
- Wind speed
- Solar position
- Time of day
- Day of year

Weather forecasts introduce uncertainty into renewable generation estimates.

Periods with low solar irradiance generally correspond to lower PV generation.

Rapid changes in irradiance can create generation variability and may require
additional operational attention.

Forecast information should therefore be interpreted together with demand,
storage state, and available grid resources.

A forecast is most useful when it supports an operational decision rather
than being treated only as a numerical prediction.
