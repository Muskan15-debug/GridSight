# Battery Energy Storage Management

Battery storage can be used to shift renewable energy from periods of high
generation to periods of low generation.

When renewable generation exceeds demand and sufficient storage capacity is
available, charging the battery can reduce unused renewable energy.

When renewable generation is below demand, stored energy can help reduce
dependence on grid electricity.

Battery decisions should consider:

- Current state of charge
- Total storage capacity
- Available charging capacity
- Expected renewable generation
- Expected energy demand
- Expected duration of the generation deficit

A battery should not be treated as an unlimited energy source.

If the expected generation deficit is larger than available stored energy,
the remaining demand may need to be supplied by the grid.

If renewable generation is expected to remain high while the battery is
already close to full capacity, additional renewable generation may need to
be exported or curtailed depending on the operating environment.

Storage planning should preserve sufficient energy for periods where
renewable generation is expected to decline.
